# The ruined gallery — artwork and provenance

Only the image files listed here were copied from the source repositories. No source application code, fonts, logos or layouts were reused. These are faithful reproductions of historical two-dimensional artworks whose creators died more than 70 years ago, used as public-domain art (including the US treatment of faithful reproductions under Bridgeman Art Library v. Corel). These are not claims that the source repositories or their other contents are CC0. CSS crops, dark overlays and frames are presentation treatments; bundled source images are unmodified.

## Caravaggio — Martha and Mary Magdalene, c. 1598

- File: `caravaggio-martha-and-mary.png`
- Artist: Michelangelo Merisi da Caravaggio (1571–1610).
- Collection: Detroit Institute of Arts.
- Work reference: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martha_and_Mary_Magdalene_(Caravaggio)
- Faithful reproduction mirror: https://github.com/teotimepacreau/View-Transitions-API-Day10-Of-100DaysOfCode/blob/40105c0f1fdfa624510cfedbe326b516080becd4/paint.png
- The mirror's `index.html` identifies this painting and its collection. The bundled reproduction was visually checked against that identification.
- Display: Black Forge mural and Blood skill-tree backdrop.

## Study of Hands — attributed to Andrea del Sarto

- File: `andrea-hand-study.jpg`
- Attribution supplied by the source filename: Andrea del Sarto (1486–1530); the filename gives 1487–1530. Exact catalogue title and date have not been independently established; the UI therefore keeps the attribution qualified.
- Faithful red-chalk drawing reproduction mirror: https://github.com/DevAdeelAhmad/RenaissanceArt/blob/bb4ba3de2c647c3c4a8356472302170a90b70c7c/assets/img/Study-of-Hands-Andrea-dsl-Sarto-1487-1530.jpg
- Display: sidebar study, Reliquary banner, Bone skill tree and subdued Chronicle background.
