# 1.18 — forged relic and hand-rolled Night Leaf

Mournfang retains the symmetrical diamond-section black-steel blade and its original animated specular sheen. The ornament palette now borrows the firearms' point-filtered weathered gunmetal and muted edge steel. Its quillons and articulated talons have a tighter angular outline, the horn crown and blood settings are smaller, and the skull receives distressed steel paint. Two ricasso collars with inset hex fasteners connect the forged blade to the more industrial fittings. Grip wraps use fewer bright metal bands. Both nine-link chains, ruby seals, bone thorns, runes, moving ruby, animated talons, thorn pommel and swing trail remain. Rig contacts, blade tip and attack timing are unchanged. The opening sword reveal uses the same model.

Night Leaf replaces the narrow straight roll with an eleven-sided, uneven hand-rolled cone: roughly 46mm across its widest stylized section, tapering to a pinched mouth end. Olive-brown leaf paint has broad mottling, branching veins, wrinkles and darker creases, plus a raised spiral seam. A ragged ash collar and charcoal chips surround the ember; small green, purple, amber and charred facets suggest packed cannabis flower at the burning face. A seven-finger green leaf mark sits on the upper wrapper. No cigarette filter is added. The roll still sits between the existing fingertips, and the smoke origin follows the new tip.

These are original low-poly meshes and procedural painted textures. All equipment remains in the native-resolution foreground pass. The environment renderer, actual material UV intoxication, healing and cooldown are unchanged. Existing checks cover 223 two-hand sword/gun poses, animated relic details, actual steel sheen, fixed smoking-arm lengths, blood/healing gameplay, and offline packaged desktop controls. In-game screenshots check the ready and raised smoking poses.
