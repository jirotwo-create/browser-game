# 1.15 — short exhale effect and tapered gunmetal firearms

The smoke effect described here is historical; version 1.16 supersedes it with [ten-second peripheral spirals](PERIPHERAL-HIGH.md). Firearm details still apply.

Night Leaf now starts a 4.2-second visual effect at the completed exhale (1.45 seconds into a puff), including cosmetic puffs at full health. An interrupted inhale does not trigger it. Strength eases in over 0.35 seconds and fades out smoothly. The twelve-second cooldown and 25-HP recovery remain.

The presentation shader adds a small, edge-weighted ripple and very slight color separation after the normal PSX world pass. The central radius stays unchanged, and gear/body/smoke are still drawn at native resolution afterward. The HUD, aim ray, camera angles, FOV, movement and enemy behavior are untouched. It introduces no blur, shake or camera roll. At peak intensity in the 960×720 GPU fixture, changed color samples stay within a few neighboring pixels; the center and gear are byte-identical to their normal frames. A zero strength restores the original output exactly. Existing affine interpolation, ground relief and color quantization remain unchanged.

The effect continues after holstering a completed puff, freezes with gameplay pause and is hidden over menus/pause/cinematics. Restart or chapter load clears it. A dead player cannot receive the effect or healing.

Both firearms now use the same 256px distressed gunmetal treatment, with oxidized patches, oily recesses, sparse worn highlights and point-filtered detail. The pistol retains its long-barrel silhouette and violet sight. Its six-sided barrel narrows from 61mm at the rear to 17mm at the tip and drifts 8mm off-center; the receiver has an unequal shoulder, side flange and opposite charging ear. The rifle's six-sided barrel narrows from 68mm to 20mm with a 12mm offset, plus unequal receiver hardware, a slimmer fore-end, a reduced open rear sight and a stock aimed back toward the shoulder. Its ready pose is lower/farther, with the support hand still on the fore-end. These are stylized mesh dimensions, not real-world firearm specifications.

Trigger contacts, fixed-length two-arm IK, fire rates, independent ammunition, reloads, empty slide lock, muzzle attachment and sword behavior remain. The new GPU/gameplay test measures actual barrel vertex spans and framebuffer effects; existing tests cover recoil/reload hand contact, visible violet sight, darker rendering, blood/healing smoke, foreground isolation and actual affine interpolation. The original opening remains available and uses the normal, unmodified presentation state.
